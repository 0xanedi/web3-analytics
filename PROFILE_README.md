## Hey, I'm 0xanedi 👋

I build **data platforms and AI systems** — the kind that take messy, high-volume,
third-party data and turn it into something a person can actually make a decision with.

Most of my work sits at the seam between the two: pipelines and modelling on one side,
retrieval and agents on the other. I care a lot about whether the numbers are *right*, and
I document what I can't prove rather than rounding it away.

---

### What I work on

**Data engineering**
- Ingestion and modelling over public and third-party APIs — typed query layers, no magic
- Analytics that reconcile: headline figures that agree with the tables underneath them
- Pragmatic quality: probe the source's real shape before trusting its docs

**AI engineering**
- Retrieval systems and RAG over messy corpora
- Agents and tool-calling workflows, with an emphasis on guardrails and cost control
- Evaluation as a first-class part of the build, not an afterthought

---

### Selected work

**[On-Chain Ledger](https://github.com/0xanedi/web3-analytics)** — seven live analytics
dashboards plus a local MCP server, built entirely on free, keyless public APIs. Every metric
is defined in code, every source is documented, and the QA notes list what's deliberately
out of scope.

> A worked example of the thing I like doing: public data → reproducible metrics →
> something a stakeholder can read in ten seconds.
